Privacy Policy

TimeGuess (package com.timeguess)
Last updated: 9 August 2026

Short version: TimeGuess does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Who we are

TimeGuess is a party game app for Android. This policy explains how the app handles information. If you have questions about it, contact us at redubbledd1@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. No personal data leaves your device, and no personal data is sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting data over a network.

Specifically, TimeGuess does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account information
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural data

3. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, TimeGuess saves a small amount of information locally in the app's private storage. This data never leaves your phone and is not accessible to other apps.

  • Player names — the nicknames you type when setting up a game, plus a short list of recently used names so you don't have to retype them.
  • Game history — results of completed games: date and time, number of players, game mode, timer settings, rounds played, and per-player scores.
  • App preferences — your chosen language, light/dark theme, and quick-play and timer settings.

Player names are entered by you and are entirely your choice. You are free to use nicknames or initials instead of real names.

4. Permissions

TimeGuess requests no runtime permissions and no internet access. It does not read or write anything outside its own app sandbox.

5. Third parties

TimeGuess contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no social media SDKs, and no crash-reporting services. No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information related to app downloads and installations independently of this app, in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. We have no access to that information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it. You are in full control:

  • Delete individual games from the History screen inside the app.
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → TimeGuess → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data.

Note that Android's automatic backup feature (allowBackup) is enabled. If you have Google Backup turned on for your device, Android may include TimeGuess's local data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your own Google account settings, and we have no access to it. You can disable device backups in your Android settings.

7. Children's privacy

TimeGuess is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data at all and transmits nothing, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU) and CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Since TimeGuess neither collects nor stores any personal data on our servers — we operate no servers — there is nothing for us to access, export, or erase. All data lives on your device and can be deleted by you at any time as described in section 6.

9. Security

All app data is kept in Android's private per-app storage, which the operating system isolates from other applications. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if a future version of the app adds an online feature — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

11. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email redubbledd1@gmail.com.
